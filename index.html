About MahaCraft SMP

Built for Gamers Across Maharashtra & India

MahaCraft SMP was started by Aashutosh Garve to bring Minecraft players together on a clean, friendly, and lag-free survival server.

Inspired by the heritage and forts of Maharashtra, our server focuses on fair survival gameplay, custom clan wars, active economy trading, and regular community events where everyone has a great time playing together.

Every rank purchase or sponsor contribution goes directly toward server hosting, DDoS protection, and building new custom features for our players.
